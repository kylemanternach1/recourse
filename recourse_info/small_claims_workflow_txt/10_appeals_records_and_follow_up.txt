Question Bank 10 - Appeals, Records, Renewals, and Closure

AI instruction
Use this bank after judgment, dismissal, settlement, or appeal activity. Ask only questions needed to track appeal rights, preserve records, monitor payment, renew judgments, or close the case.

Stage goal
Protect deadlines and keep the case record complete through appeal, payment, renewal, or satisfaction.

Model knowledge to apply when interpreting answers
- Appeal rights in small claims are limited and depend on party role and whether the judgment was on the plaintiff's claim or a defendant's claim.
- The appeal deadline usually runs from mailing of the Notice of Entry of Judgment, so the AI should ask for the SC-130 mailing date.
- An appeal can delay enforcement and may result in a new hearing where evidence must be presented again.
- Good recordkeeping matters for appeal, collection, satisfaction, credit disputes, and future renewal.
- If the judgment remains unpaid, the user may need long-term tracking for interest, payments, enforcement costs, and renewal deadlines.
- If the judgment is paid, the user should file satisfaction promptly and address any liens or collection actions.
- The workflow should end with one of these statuses: closed satisfied, closed dismissed, appeal pending, collection pending, renewal monitoring, or not suitable for small claims.

Always extract first if available
- SC-130 mailing date
- party role
- judgment result
- counterclaim status
- appeal status
- payment status
- satisfaction status

High-priority questions
- Did you receive SC-130 Notice of Entry of Judgment?
- What date was SC-130 mailed?
- Were you the plaintiff, defendant, or both because of a counterclaim?
- Are you considering an appeal?
- Did the other side appeal?
- Was there a counterclaim judgment?
- Has the judgment been fully paid?
- Do you need copies of filed forms, proofs of service, orders, exhibits, or judgment documents?

Waiting-state questions
- Are you waiting to see whether the other side appeals?
- Are you waiting for an appeal hearing date?
- Are you waiting for payments under a judgment or settlement?
- Are you waiting for court copies, certified copies, or conformed documents?
- Are you waiting for a lien release or satisfaction to be processed?
- Do you need a future reminder before the judgment renewal deadline?

Conditional questions
Ask if user is considering appeal:
- Are you appealing a claim you were allowed to appeal, or do you need help determining appeal rights?
- What deadline appears to apply based on the SC-130 mailing date?
- Do you need a checklist for SC-140 Notice of Appeal?

Ask if the other side appealed:
- Did you receive a notice of appeal?
- What is the new hearing date, if any?
- Do you still have all exhibits and witnesses available?

Ask if there was a counterclaim:
- Did the judgment involve the plaintiff's claim, the defendant's claim, or both?
- Which party wants to appeal which part?

Ask if judgment is unpaid:
- Do you want payment reminders?
- Do you want a renewal reminder before the judgment expires?
- Are you tracking interest and enforcement costs?

Ask if judgment is fully paid:
- Has an Acknowledgment of Satisfaction of Judgment been filed?
- Were any liens recorded that need release?
- Do you need a final case closure packet?

Ask if case records are incomplete:
- Do you have the filed SC-100 and attachments?
- Do you have proof of service?
- Do you have fee waiver orders or receipts?
- Do you have SC-130 or judgment documents?
- Do you have enforcement documents, if any?

Do not ask
- Do not ask enforcement asset questions unless the judgment is unpaid and enforcement is being considered.
- Do not ask initial eligibility questions unless the user is starting a new claim.

Validation logic
- Appeal deadlines usually run from mailing of SC-130.
- Appeal rights differ depending on party role and whether a counterclaim was involved.
- Appeal may delay enforcement.
- Judgment records should be preserved for collection, appeal, credit correction, and proof of satisfaction.
- Unpaid judgments may require renewal tracking.
- Paid judgments require satisfaction filing.

Risk flags
- appeal_deadline_unclear
- appeal_rights_uncertain
- sc_130_missing
- records_incomplete
- renewal_deadline_needed
- satisfaction_not_filed
- lien_release_needed

Response behavior
- If appeal rights are unclear, ask party-role and counterclaim questions before giving an appeal path.
- If no appeal is filed by the deadline, move the case to final/enforceable status.
- If appeal is filed, preserve exhibits and prepare for a new hearing rather than collection.
- If the case is paid, generate satisfaction and closure steps.
- If the case remains unpaid, connect to the enforcement question bank and create renewal monitoring.

Stage completion standard
Stop asking appeal and follow-up questions when deadlines are tracked, the case file is complete, and the case is either in appeal, collection, renewal monitoring, or closed.

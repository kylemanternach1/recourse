Question Bank 04 - Court Forms Packet

AI instruction
Use this bank to collect only the missing fields needed for the official form packet. Do not ask questions already answered in prior stages unless the field must be confirmed exactly as it will appear on a Judicial Council form.

Stage goal
Prepare SC-100 and any required companion forms or attachments.

Model knowledge to apply when interpreting answers
- California small claims filing is form-driven. SC-100 is the core plaintiff claim form.
- SC-100 should contain concise facts, not a long legal brief. The story should usually include date, relationship/transaction, what defendant did or failed to do, and amount lost.
- Extra parties may require SC-100A.
- Fictitious business name issues may require SC-103 or county-specific support.
- Fee waiver requests are separate from the main claim and should be treated as confidential fee-related paperwork.
- Court forms should use exact party names from the venue/party verification step.
- The AI should not finalize a form packet if required fields are missing, if the amount does not match the damages sheet, or if local county forms have not been checked.
- The AI should flag signature fields because the user, not the AI, must sign under penalty of perjury.

Always extract first if available
- plaintiff name and contact information
- defendant name and service address
- claim amount
- claim narrative
- additional parties
- fictitious business name status
- fee waiver need

High-priority questions
- Please confirm the plaintiff name exactly as it should appear on the court form.
- What is the plaintiff's mailing address?
- What phone number and email should appear on the form, if any?
- Please confirm each defendant name exactly as it should appear on the court form.
- What address should be listed for each defendant?
- Is the amount on the form exactly the same as the damages total we calculated?
- Is the short claim statement accurate and factual?
- Are you requesting a fee waiver?

Conditional questions
Ask if additional parties exist:
- Which plaintiffs or defendants do not fit in the main SC-100 fields?
- For each additional party, what is the exact name, address, and role?

Ask if a business or DBA is involved:
- Is the plaintiff doing business under a fictitious business name?
- Is the defendant doing business under a fictitious business name?
- Do you have the fictitious business name statement or registration information?

Ask if claim narrative is too long:
- Can we shorten the explanation to the key dates, promise, breach/wrong, and amount lost?
- Should a separate attachment be used for the claim explanation?

Ask if fee waiver may apply:
- Do you want to prepare a Request to Waive Court Fees?
- Are you receiving public benefits, low income, or unable to pay court fees and basic needs?

Ask if county local forms may be needed:
- Which county are you filing in?
- Has the county small claims page listed any local forms or cover sheets?

Do not ask
- Do not ask for broad story details again if a claim narrative already exists.
- Do not ask for post-judgment information at this stage.

Validation logic
- Use current official Judicial Council forms.
- SC-100 is the main plaintiff claim form.
- SC-100A may be needed for additional plaintiffs or defendants.
- SC-103 may be needed for fictitious business name issues.
- FW-001 may be needed for fee waiver.
- Amount must match damages computation.
- Party names must match verified legal names.
- Signature/date fields must be flagged for user completion.

Risk flags
- party_name_unverified
- defendant_address_missing
- amount_mismatch
- outdated_form_version
- missing_attachment
- local_form_needed
- signature_required

Response behavior
- If the packet is ready, produce a filing checklist and identify what the user must sign.
- If the packet is not ready, list only the missing fields that block filing.
- If the claim narrative is too long, rewrite it into a short factual statement and ask the user to confirm accuracy.
- If county local requirements are unknown, mark the packet as draft-ready but not filing-ready.

Data fields to populate
- sc_100_fields
- sc_100a_fields
- sc_103_fields
- fw_001_needed
- local_forms_needed
- attachments_list
- item_3_claim_statement
- signature_required
- form_packet_status

Stage completion standard
Stop asking forms questions when every required form field is populated or clearly marked as missing, and the AI can generate a form-completeness checklist.

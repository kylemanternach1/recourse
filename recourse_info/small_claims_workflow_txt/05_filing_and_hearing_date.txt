Question Bank 05 - Filing, Fees, and Hearing Date

AI instruction
Use this bank after the form packet is ready. Ask only questions needed to select a filing method, estimate fees, track acceptance or rejection, and capture the hearing date.

Stage goal
Get the claim filed in the correct county and record the case number, hearing date, and service deadlines.

Model knowledge to apply when interpreting answers
- Filing starts the court case, but it does not mean the defendant has been notified. Service is a separate required step.
- Counties vary in filing methods. Some allow e-filing, mail, drop box, fax, or in-person filing; the AI must verify local rules before giving final instructions.
- After filing, the clerk should return a file-stamped/conformed copy with a case number and hearing information. That file-stamped version is what gets served.
- Filing fees depend on the claim amount and sometimes the filer history. Fee waivers may be available for eligible users.
- If filing is rejected or returned, the AI should treat the rejection reason as the next workflow input and route the user back to the missing/corrected item.
- After the hearing date is assigned, the AI must calculate service deadlines and explain that missing service deadlines can cause continuance, dismissal, or inability to get judgment.
- The user may wait after filing for court acceptance, conformed copies, fee waiver decisions, or hearing notices. The AI should track each wait state.

Always extract first if available
- selected county
- filing method preference
- fee waiver status
- case number
- hearing date
- rejection reason

High-priority questions
- Which county are you filing in?
- Do you want to file in person, by mail, drop box, fax, or e-filing if available?
- Are you requesting a fee waiver?
- Has the court accepted the filing?
- What case number did the court assign?
- What is the hearing date and time?
- What courthouse, department, or remote hearing information did the court provide?
- Did you receive a file-stamped copy of SC-100?

Waiting-state questions
- Are you still waiting for the court to accept or reject the filing?
- Are you still waiting for a conformed/file-stamped copy?
- Are you still waiting for the fee waiver decision?
- Are you still waiting for the hearing notice?
- Did the court give any deadline to fix a filing defect or pay a fee?

Conditional questions
Ask if filing by mail:
- Do you have the original, required copies, and return envelope if the court requires one?
- What mailing address does the county court use for small claims filings?

Ask if filing electronically:
- Which e-filing portal does the county use?
- Are all documents saved as separate PDFs if required?
- Did the e-filing system return, reject, or accept the filing?

Ask if fee waiver requested:
- Did the court grant, deny, or defer the fee waiver?
- Did you receive an order on the fee waiver?
- If denied, what payment deadline did the court give?

Ask if filing was rejected or returned:
- What exact reason did the clerk or e-filing system give?
- Which form, attachment, fee, signature, or venue issue needs correction?

Ask once hearing date is known:
- Is each defendant in the filing county or outside the filing county?
- Based on defendant location, what is the last safe date for service?

Do not ask
- Do not ask service-method details until a file-stamped claim exists.
- Do not ask hearing preparation questions until hearing date and service status are known.

Validation logic
- Filing county must match the venue analysis.
- Filing method must be accepted by the county.
- Filing fee should match the claim amount and filer history.
- Fee waiver status must be tracked separately.
- The file-stamped SC-100 is the version to serve.
- Service deadlines must be calculated immediately after hearing date is known.

Risk flags
- county_filing_method_unverified
- filing_rejected
- fee_waiver_denied
- case_number_missing
- hearing_date_missing
- file_stamped_copy_missing
- service_deadline_approaching

Response behavior
- If filing has not happened, provide county-specific filing options and what to submit.
- If filing is pending, explain what the user is waiting for and what status to check.
- If filing was rejected, identify the correction path and route back to the relevant question bank.
- If filing was accepted, congratulate briefly, then immediately move to service deadlines and service planning.

Stage completion standard
Stop asking filing questions when the filing is accepted, the hearing date is known, and the user has a file-stamped copy ready for service.

Question Bank 08 - Hearing Attendance and Judgment Result

AI instruction
Use this bank on hearing day or after the hearing. Ask only questions needed to prepare final attendance reminders or capture the court's result and next deadline.

Stage goal
Record whether the case was heard, continued, dismissed, settled, defaulted, or reduced to judgment.

Model knowledge to apply when interpreting answers
- The hearing may not end with immediate payment. The court may rule at the hearing, mail a decision later, continue the case, dismiss it, or approve settlement.
- If the defendant does not appear, the user does not automatically win unless service is valid and the judge accepts the proof.
- If the case is continued, the user must understand what action is required before the new date.
- If the case is dismissed without prejudice, the user may be able to fix the problem and refile or continue; if dismissed with prejudice, the issue is more serious.
- SC-130 Notice of Entry of Judgment is important because it starts appeal and enforcement timing.
- After judgment, there is usually a waiting period before enforcement. The AI should not move directly to garnishment or levy without checking deadlines and appeals.

Always extract first if available
- hearing date/time
- court location or remote link
- attendance status
- defendant appearance
- hearing result
- judgment amount
- SC-130 status

Pre-hearing questions
- What time is the hearing?
- Is the hearing in person, remote, or hybrid?
- Do you have the courthouse department or remote appearance link?
- Do you have photo ID?
- Do you have the filed claim, proof of service, exhibit packet, witness list, and hearing script?
- Are your witnesses present or available?
- Are you prepared to discuss settlement or mediation if offered?

Post-hearing questions
- Did you attend the hearing?
- Did the defendant attend?
- Did the judge hear the case, continue it, dismiss it, enter default, or approve a settlement?
- If the case was continued, what is the new date and why was it continued?
- If judgment was entered, who won?
- What amount was awarded?
- Were filing fees, service fees, or other costs awarded?
- Did the judge order payment by a certain date or in installments?
- Did you receive SC-130 Notice of Entry of Judgment?
- If not, did the court say it would mail the decision?

Waiting-state questions
- Did the judge take the case under submission and say a decision would be mailed later?
- Are you waiting for SC-130 Notice of Entry of Judgment?
- Are you waiting to see whether the defendant appeals?
- Are you waiting for the defendant to make voluntary payment?
- Did the court set a future compliance, payment, or continued hearing date?

Conditional questions
Ask if defendant did not appear:
- Was proof of service accepted by the court?
- Did the court enter default judgment or require another step?

Ask if case was dismissed:
- Was the dismissal with prejudice or without prejudice?
- What reason did the court give?
- Can the issue be fixed by service, venue correction, better party naming, or refiling?

Ask if case was continued:
- What must be completed before the new hearing date?
- Is new or corrected service required?
- Did the court order exchange or submission of documents?

Ask if settlement occurred:
- Are the settlement terms written?
- How much will be paid, by what date, and what happens if payment is missed?

Ask if judgment entered:
- What date was judgment entered or mailed?
- What is the appeal or waiting-period deadline?

Do not ask
- Do not ask collection questions until it is clear the user won a money judgment and the waiting period is relevant.

Validation logic
- Default judgment depends on valid proof of service.
- Dismissal reason determines whether refiling is possible.
- SC-130 starts important post-judgment deadlines.
- Enforcement usually waits until the appeal period expires.
- Settlement terms should be written and specific.

Risk flags
- missed_hearing
- proof_of_service_rejected
- dismissal_reason_unclear
- continuance_requirements_unclear
- judgment_amount_unclear
- sc_130_missing
- settlement_terms_not_written

Response behavior
- If the result is unclear, ask for the exact wording on the minute order, SC-130, or court notice.
- If the user won, explain the waiting period and payment/appeal monitoring before enforcement.
- If the user lost, explain appeal availability cautiously based on party role and counterclaim status.
- If the case was continued, generate a new task list tied to the continuation reason.
- If settlement was reached, track payment dates and what happens if payment is missed.

Data fields to populate
- attendance_status
- defendant_appearance_status
- hearing_result
- continuance_date
- dismissal_type
- dismissal_reason
- judgment_winner
- judgment_amount
- costs_awarded
- payment_terms
- sc_130_received
- sc_130_mailing_date
- settlement_terms
- next_deadline
- decision_wait_state
- voluntary_payment_wait_state
- appeal_wait_state

Stage completion standard
Stop asking hearing-result questions when the AI can state what happened in court and what the user's next step and deadline are.

# Filing Guide: California Small Claims (SC-100)

## Limits

- Individuals: up to **$12,500** per claim.
- Sole proprietors: same $12,500 limit.
- Businesses/entities: up to **$6,250** per claim.
- You can file up to two claims over $2,500 per calendar year.

## Required forms

- **SC-100** Plaintiff's Claim and ORDER to Go to Small Claims Court.
- One copy per defendant + one for the court + one for your records.

## Filing fees (typical)

- Claims up to $1,500: **$30**
- $1,500.01 – $5,000: **$50**
- $5,000.01 – $12,500: **$75**

If you cannot afford the fee, you may submit form **FW-001** for a fee waiver.

## Venue

You generally file in the county where:

- The defendant lives or does business, or
- The incident or contract dispute happened, or
- The damaged property is located.

## After filing

1. Court schedules a hearing 30–70 days out.
2. Serve each defendant at least **15 days** before the hearing (20 days if outside the county).
3. Service must be by an adult who is not a party (sheriff, process server, certified mail by clerk, or any non-party adult).
4. File **SC-104** Proof of Service before the hearing.

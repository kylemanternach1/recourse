# Phase 02 — Venue & parties

Lock the correct county and exact legal names before filling SC-100.

## Venue (pick one basis)

- Defendant lives or does business in the county
- Where the transaction happened
- Where injury/damage occurred
- Where contract was to be performed
- Consumer protections may allow plaintiff's county

## Parties

- [ ] Plaintiff full legal name and mailing address
- [ ] Defendant full legal name (individual, business, or sole proprietor)
- [ ] If business: verify registered agent / SOS lookup
- [ ] DBA / fictitious name → plan SC-103
- [ ] More than two defendants → plan SC-100A

## When done

Mark `parties_venue` complete via `update_case_state`.

# Phase 09 — Enforcement

Only if plaintiff won a **money judgment** and defendant has not paid.

## Before involuntary collection

- Wait **30 days** after judgment for appeal window (unless court says otherwise)
- Track voluntary payments in a ledger

## Tools

- **SC-133** — ask court to order debtor's financial statement
- Wage garnishment, bank levy, property lien (abstract of judgment)
- Debtor examination

## Outputs

- `draft/post-judgment-plan.md` — chosen tools, deadlines, amounts

## When paid in full

File Acknowledgment of Satisfaction of Judgment; move to closure phase.

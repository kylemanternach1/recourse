# SC-100 Intake Questions

Collect each of these from the plaintiff. Save responses to `intake.md` in the workspace.

## Parties

1. Plaintiff full legal name, address, phone, and email.
2. Are you suing as an individual, sole proprietor, partnership, or corporation?
3. Defendant 1 full legal name and best mailing address.
4. Is Defendant 1 an individual or a business? If business, what kind?
5. Are there additional defendants? Capture the same details for each.

## The claim

6. In plain language, what happened? (3–5 sentences.)
7. When did it happen? (Specific dates.)
8. Where did it happen? (City and county.)
9. Why does the defendant owe you money or owe you something?
10. Have you asked the defendant to pay or fix the problem? What did they say?

## Amount

11. How much money are you asking for? (Total in dollars.)
12. List each cost or loss that adds up to that total.
13. Do you have receipts, photos, contracts, or messages to support each item? List them.

## Venue

14. Where does the defendant live or do business?
15. Where did the events in question happen?

# Phase 08 — Hearing & judgment

Capture what happened at the hearing.

## Bring

- Photo ID
- File-stamped claim, proof of service, exhibits
- `draft/hearing-script.md`

## Record outcome

- Judgment for plaintiff (amount, costs)
- Continuance (new date, what to do before)
- Dismissal (with/without prejudice)
- Default (defendant did not appear)
- Settlement on the record

## After hearing

- Obtain **SC-130** Notice of Entry of Judgment when available
- Note payment terms if ordered
- Set `waiting` for SC-130 or voluntary payment

Optional: `draft/hearing-outcome.md`
